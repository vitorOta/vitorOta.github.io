How it works

Therapy, step by step

What the first session is like

In our first session, you bring the issues you would like to work on. You are free to share your life story and whatever is troubling you most at that moment. I ask a few guiding questions that are part of the initial assessment and outline some hypotheses that will shape your treatment plan.

In my sessions, I always invite you to bring something to drink (tea, coffee, water; anything non-alcoholic is fine) and some tissues for support. This brings a little more care to our time together.
